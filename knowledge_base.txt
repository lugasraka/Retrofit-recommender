# Expert Knowledge Base for Industrial Equipment Retrofit Recommendations

## Voltage Mismatch Issues
CRITICAL: Voltage mismatches between controllers and actuators can cause equipment damage, fire hazards, and system failures.
- 230V controller with 24V actuator: Actuator will burn out. Immediate safety shutdown required.
- 24V controller with 230V actuator: Actuator won't function. No safety risk but complete failure.
- Solution: Always match controller output voltage to actuator input voltage (SAX31.00 for 230V, SAX61.03 for 24V).
- Safety Alert: MANDATORY for any voltage mismatch detected.

## Sensor Calibration and Drift
Sensors drift over time, especially temperature sensors. Signs include:
- Readings varying ±2°C or more from reference
- Inconsistent measurements between identical sensors
- Sensors older than 5 years without calibration
Solutions:
- SERVICE-CALIB: Professional calibration with certificate (€120 per sensor)
- Replacement: New sensors if beyond calibration (QAE2120.010 for duct, QAA75.110 for outdoor)
- Preventive: Annual calibration recommended for critical sensors

## Pressure Sensor Applications
Differential pressure sensors critical for:
- Filter monitoring (QBM2030-1: 0-1000 Pa for air filters)
- Pump performance (QBE2103-P25: 0-25 bar for water systems)
- System balance verification
- Energy optimization (pressure-based control)
Failure symptoms: Out of range readings, system bypass active, no flow measurement

## Actuator Selection Guide
Torque requirements:
- Standard valves DN25-DN65: 20Nm actuators (SAX31.00, SAX61.03)
- Large valves DN80-DN100: 50Nm heavy duty (SAX81.00, SAX81.03)
- Ball valves: 5Nm on/off (GLB161.1E)
- Dampers: 10Nm modulating (AGK161.9E)
Signal types:
- 0-10V: Analog modulating control (SAX61.03, SAX81.03)
- 3-point: Simple floating control (SAX31.00, SAX81.00)
- On/Off: Binary control (GLB161.1E)

## Common Failure Patterns
Pattern 1: Pump overworking + high flow resistance = Valve issue (recommend VVF53.65-ECO or BUNDLE-VALVE-ACT)
Pattern 2: Actuator failed + voltage output mentioned = Voltage mismatch (match voltage + safety alert)
Pattern 3: Inconsistent readings + sensor age >5 years = Sensor drift (SERVICE-CALIB or replacement)
Pattern 4: Cannot integrate with BMS = Legacy controller (RWD68 or RWF55.50E9)
Pattern 5: Long runtime without service = Preventive maintenance needed (SERVICE-PM)
Pattern 6: Multiple aging components = System retrofit (SERVICE-RETROFIT or BUNDLE-ENERGY-OPT)
